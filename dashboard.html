Segment rules. Champions: spend ₹2.5L or more, satisfaction 7 or higher, member for 6+ years. Rising Value: spend ₹2.5L or more, satisfaction 5 to 10, not yet a Champion. At-Risk High Spenders: spend ₹2.5L or more, satisfaction 4 or lower. Loyal Mid-Spenders: ₹1L to 2.5L, 6+ years. Casual Mid-Spenders: ₹1L to 2.5L, under 6 years. Low Value: under ₹1L.

Why value segments instead of customer type. On the full dataset, an ANOVA found no significant difference between New, Returning and Premium in spending (p = 0.26), purchases (p = 0.07), satisfaction (p = 0.68) or membership years (p = 0.15). K-Means clustering also found no natural groups (silhouette about 0.15), so segments here are rule-based.

Data checks worth raising. 1,570 customers labelled New have 5 or more membership years, and Feedback does not follow Satisfaction_Score (590 customers have Excellent feedback with a score of 3 or lower). Treat tenure and sentiment findings with care until the source is confirmed.
